Questions? Answered

Frequently asked questions

Everything about buying, renewing and claiming your vehicle insurance.

Is third-party vehicle insurance mandatory in India?
Yes. Under the Motor Vehicles Act, 1988, every vehicle on Indian roads must carry at least a valid third-party liability policy. Driving without one is a punishable offence.
What does a third-party policy actually cover?
It covers injury, death or property damage you cause to a third party, plus a mandatory ₹15 lakh personal-accident cover for the owner. It's the legally required minimum for every vehicle in India.
How quickly can I get my policy?
Usually within minutes. Share your vehicle and contact details, get an indicative quote instantly, and our advisor confirms and issues the IRDAI-compliant e-policy to your inbox.
How do I file or track a claim?
Message us on WhatsApp with your policy number, or call your advisor. Our 24×7 claims desk guides you through survey, documentation and settlement.
Can I insure a commercial vehicle or a fleet?
Yes — trucks, taxis, autos, buses, tractors and full fleets. Fleet policies come with consolidated billing and a dedicated relationship manager.
Will my No Claim Bonus (NCB) carry over if I switch?
Yes. Your accumulated NCB (up to 50%) transfers when you renew or move to a new insurer through Bharat Insurance.
What documents do I need?
Usually just your vehicle registration (RC) and a mobile number to start. For own-damage cover a quick photo self-inspection may be needed if the policy has lapsed.
